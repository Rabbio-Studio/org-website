FAQ

Common questions about our bootcamps.

Do I need prior experience to join a bootcamp?

No, many of our bootcamps are designed for beginners with no prior coding experience. We'll teach you everything you need to know from the ground up.

What kind of job support do you provide?

We offer comprehensive career services including resume building, interview preparation, portfolio reviews, and direct connections to our hiring partners.

Are there financing options available?

Yes, we offer various financing options including payment plans, scholarships, and partnerships with lending institutions. Contact us for more details.

What is the time commitment for bootcamps?

Our full-time bootcamps typically run for 12-20 weeks, Monday to Friday, from 9 AM to 5 PM. Part-time options may also be available for some programs.

Can I attend remotely?

Yes, all our bootcamps are available in a fully remote format, allowing you to learn from anywhere in the world with a stable internet connection.
